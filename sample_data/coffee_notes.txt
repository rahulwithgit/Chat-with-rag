Coffee brewing basics

Use fresh beans and grind just before brewing. For pour-over, aim for water
between 92 and 96 degrees Celsius. A common starting ratio is 1 gram of coffee
to 16 grams of water. Bloom the grounds with a small pour for about 30 seconds
before continuing the pour in slow circles.

Storage

Keep beans in an airtight container away from light and heat. Freezing can work
for long-term storage if the bag is truly sealed to avoid moisture and odors.
